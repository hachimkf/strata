Welcome to Strata

Every version of your design work, gathered by client and project.
  • Finds every version: renamed copies, “(Recovered)” files after a crash, exports in Downloads.
  • Groups by client: Norvia, Velora… each with its projects and timelines.
  • Opens the latest in one press: ⇧⌘B from any app.
  • Compare, tag and deliver: side-by-side versions, Final / Sent tags, client brand kits.
Strata reads your Desktop, Documents and Downloads and works offline. It never moves or edits your files on its own. macOS will ask once for access to those folders.
